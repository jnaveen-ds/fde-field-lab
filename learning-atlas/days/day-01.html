PHASE 01 · DISCOVERY

Start with the outcome.
Not the agent.

An FDE owns the distance between an ambiguous customer problem and a reliable, measurable result. Technology is selected after the problem and constraints are understood.

DAY01Role · baseline · outcome
01

THE CORE DISTINCTION

Output is what we make.
Outcome is what improves.

OUTPUT

“The agent generated a reply.”

  • model responded
  • workflow completed
  • feature exists
Necessary, but not proof of value.
≠
OUTCOME

“The agent helped approve a useful response faster and safely.”

  • customer workflow improved
  • quality guardrail held
  • result was measured
This is the reason to build.
Example: A beautiful multi-agent demo with no users, no baseline, and no measured improvement is an output—not an FDE outcome.
02

THE ROLE

Four responsibilities.
One accountable owner.

01

Discover

Observe workflows, interview stakeholders, separate facts from assumptions, and identify the costly constraint.

Artifact: discovery evidence
02

Build

Deliver the smallest end-to-end solution across UI, API, data, integration, and AI where justified.

Artifact: tested vertical slice
03

Operate

Protect data, control actions, instrument failures, deploy safely, and support the system in its real environment.

Artifact: traces and runbook
04

Measure

Track adoption and impact, learn from rejection and failure, then turn field feedback into the next iteration.

Artifact: before/after result
FDE=customer translator+full-stack builder+production operator+outcome partner

05

OUR CAPSTONE

Anatomy of a useful
customer outcome.

For Northstar support agents, reduce the median time from ticket arrival to an approved, evidence-backed first useful response, without reducing correct routing or permitting unauthorized external actions.
WHOSupport agent

The person whose workflow must improve.

CHANGEReduce elapsed time

The direction of measurable improvement.

QUALITYEvidence + routing

Speed cannot trade away correctness.

SAFETYNo unauthorized action

Approval remains outside model judgment.

Important:

This outcome is still a hypothesis. We will not invent a percentage target before discovery and baseline measurement.

06

WHY COMPANIES CARE

Same discipline.
Different operating context.

MNC

Coordination and governance

  • many stakeholders and approval paths
  • existing identity, data, and platform standards
  • formal security and architecture reviews
  • change management and measurable rollout

Day 1 prevents local technical success from conflicting with enterprise constraints.

STARTUP

Focus and learning speed

  • limited time, runway, and engineering capacity
  • rapid customer feedback and changing priorities
  • simple architecture and short delivery cycles
  • clear unit economics and adoption signals

Day 1 prevents fast execution on a low-value problem.
